# Overall Vision

The game should feel like a polished indie platformer that teaches through experience rather than reading.

Players should naturally learn by:

* Exploring
* Platforming
* Talking to NPCs
* Solving puzzles
* Completing small tasks
* Discovering collectibles
* Observing environmental changes
* Making simple choices
* Participating in mini-games
* Experiencing cause-and-effect scenarios

The experience should always prioritize:

* Fun first
* Learning second

Never overwhelm players with long paragraphs of text.

Instead, deliver information through small, meaningful interactions.
